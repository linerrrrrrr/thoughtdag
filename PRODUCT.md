# ThoughtDAG

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

ThoughtDAG turns LLM conversations into editable directed acyclic graphs on an infinite canvas. Nodes contain question-and-answer turns; edges carry context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19, TypeScript, Vite, React Flow, Zustand, IndexedDB, and an Express LLM proxy.
- Preserve graph editing, attachments, branching, selection, semantic zoom, focus panels, and bilingual Chinese/English copy.
- Layout follows context arrows. Vertical chains share a column from top to bottom; horizontal chains share a top-aligned row from left to right. Connection ports adapt to geometry without changing what the edge carries.
- This is an actively developed open prototype.

## Brand Commitments

- The user rejected mismatched rounded outlines and straight accents, then clarified that rounded corners are welcome when they form an aesthetically coherent system. Do not turn this into an all-square interface.
- Keep the start node's left-border highlight integrated into its rounded outline; preserve distinct note, file, and link material identities at reduced zoom, prioritizing readable names and actual file-extension cues.
- UI text avoids promotional third-party brand mentions; functional identifiers and actual data sources retain their names.

## Evidence on Hand

AGENTS.md, the existing application, and the user's screenshot establish the product and the geometry problem. No additional audience or commercial claims are assumed.
